Get ready for your doctor visit in a few minutes

Let's get you ready

Answer a few short questions about how you're feeling. We'll turn your answers into a one-page summary you can bring to your appointment.

This app does not give medical advice or a diagnosis. It only helps you organize what you want to tell your doctor.

Your answers stay on this device. Nothing is saved after you close this tab.

Step 1 of 6

What's the main reason for your visit?

Say it in your own words. For example: "My knee has been hurting" or "I need a refill on my blood pressure medicine."

Step 2 of 6

When did it start?

A rough idea is fine.

Step 3 of 6

What makes it better or worse?

Not sure? Leave it blank — that's okay.

Step 4 of 6

What medications are you currently taking?

Include prescriptions, vitamins, and anything you buy without a prescription.

Step 5 of 6

What have you already tried?

e.g., rest, ice, over-the-counter medicine, a heating pad

Step 6 of 6

What do you want to ask the doctor?

List up to 3 questions. Put your most important one first — a 15-minute visit goes fast.
